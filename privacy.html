Privacy Policy

App: Calculator: Clean & Fast (com.calculatorapp) · Last Updated: November 6, 2026

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Calculator: Clean & Fast ("the App"), developed by Nirali Motka, is designed from the ground up as a private, offline-first calculation utility. We firmly believe that standard mathematical tools have zero justification for tracking users, profiling behavior, or harvesting personal data.

We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) whatsoever.

2. Data Collection & Permissions

The App operates with strictly minimal device permissions:

  • No Account Required: You never need to log in, register, or provide an email, phone number, or identity.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: The App does not integrate third-party ad networks, trackers, Firebase Analytics, or user profiling SDKs.
  • No Device Identifier Collection: We do not read or store Android Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, MAC address, or serial numbers.
  • Camera, Contacts, Location: Never requested. The App will never request access to your camera, photos, contacts, location, or microphone.

3. Local On-Device Storage

All calculations, history tape logs, custom memory values (M+, M-, MR), and unit conversion preferences are saved 100% locally on your device using encrypted Android sandbox storage (AsyncStorage).

This data never leaves your device. You can clear your calculation history at any moment via the in-app "Clear History" button or by clearing the app data in Android system settings.

4. Network Connectivity & Currency Rates

The App includes a multi-currency converter feature. To provide accurate conversions:

  • The App makes an encrypted HTTPS query to public currency exchange rate APIs to download current foreign exchange rates.
  • No user credentials, location data, or identifiers are transmitted with these queries.
  • Exchange rate data is cached locally so conversions continue functioning seamlessly even when offline.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The App is a general-purpose mathematical tool suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13, in strict adherence to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

6. Security & Data Retention

Because all application data remains in local on-device application sandboxes, the security of your stored calculation history is protected by standard Android operating system security and filesystem encryption.
